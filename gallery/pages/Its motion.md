## Its motion
The shape turns once every $56\,\mathrm{s}$, $\varphi(t) = 2\pi t / 56$, and its lobes breathe: $\varepsilon(t) = 0.22 + 0.035\sin(0.35\,t)$.

The faint rings are the same curve at larger scales, with $\varepsilon$ shrinking outward and turning the other way.
