## The trefoil
A closed curve whose radius swells three times in every turn.

$$r(\theta) = R\,\bigl(1 + \varepsilon\cos 3(\theta - \varphi)\bigr), \qquad \varepsilon \approx 0.22$$

---

Turning it by $2\pi/3$ gives the same curve, so its rotations form the cyclic group $C_3$; with its three mirrors it has the symmetry of $D_3$.

> [!mir|nucleus] The centre
> The one point every symmetry fixes, $z = 0$.

> [!mir|tip-0] A lobe
> Where $\cos 3(\theta - \varphi) = 1$ the radius peaks at $R(1 + \varepsilon)$.

> [!mir|tip-1] Curvature at a tip
> $\kappa = \dfrac{1 + 10\varepsilon}{R\,(1 + \varepsilon)^2} \approx 2.15/R$

> [!mir|@0.39+0.68i] The waist
> Between two lobes the radius dips to $R(1 - \varepsilon)$.
