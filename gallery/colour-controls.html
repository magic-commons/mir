COLOUR CONTROLS

An arc is a colour; a knob is a parameter; a slider is a thing's one principal parameter. The arc, the swatch and the lane slider answer the hand the kit's one law gives every control: a vertical drag, any modifier or a second finger for an eighth, a double-tap for home.

ARC KNOB

A hue, a phase, an angle: no body, a round-capped ring in its own ink. A bounded arc has the 60° gap at the bottom; a free one is the whole ring. The angular law (HUE below) is finer the farther from the hub the hand is.

HUE SWATCH

The colour itself. Tap: the platform's chooser. Press and drag 8 px up or down: its hue turns, saturation and value kept.

LANE SLIDER

A long pill in the lane's ink with a glowing thumb: a colour's frequency, a layer's gain. Horizontal or vertical.

ROUTED

All four are parameters of the modulation window. The arcs keep the base the hand owns and ride the live value as a dot; the pill draws the window's RANGE bar and a live dot.

CHIP STRIP

The rail's round discs in the flow, beside a lane or a pane. The × is armed: it asks twice.

SORTABLE LIST

One pane per item, the chips on the rack's outer edge. Drag the grip or use the arrow keys; × asks twice; + ADD dims at the cap (5).
